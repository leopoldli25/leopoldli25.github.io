Penguin Party

Penguin Party is a multiplayer party and co-op extraction game for 1 to 8 players, made in Unity and C#. It has 6 different modes, including co-op extraction, baseball, and a party board game with 50 minigames. To keep online play fair even with lag, every mode is either turn-based, simulated the same way on every machine from a shared match seed, or played in separate lanes for each player.

Polygon Paradise

Polygon Paradise is a city-builder game with a simple art style. Build your base and upgrade buildings to generate resources. Unlock, upgrade, and equip polygons to make them stronger and use them in the procedurally generated dungeon to get rewards. Watch them battle in a physics-based autobattler

TND Card Game

This is a 1v1 card game with online multiplayer with over 30 unique cards. Includes a functional deckbuilder and the ability to easily import and export decklists. Connect with other players by entering a code such as "ABCD" and clicking host, and then have them enter the same code and click connect.

Minimize Loss

This is a simple minigame that I helped make for the EGaDS Mega Micro Jam 2022. This game is a take on the famous Trolley Problem with the goal being to save as many people as possible by quickly choosing the path with the least people.
