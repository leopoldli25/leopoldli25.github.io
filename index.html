Games

Penguin Party

Multiplayer party and co-op extraction game, 1 to 8 players. Unity, C#.

Six modes in one game, including co-op extraction, baseball, and a party board game with 50 minigames. Online play was laggy in early tests and players hated it, so I made a rule for every mode: it has to be turn-based, simulated the same way on every machine from a shared match seed, or played in separate lanes for each player. That rule shaped every mode I added after.

I also wrote the design pillars and a short premise brief, and I checked every new minigame, hazard, and item against them so the 50 minigames would still feel like one game.

Polygon Paradise

City builder with a physics-based autobattler. Unity, C#.

Build your base and upgrade buildings to make resources. Unlock, upgrade, and equip polygons, then send them into a procedurally generated dungeon where they fight on their own. Enemies change their strategy based on how earlier battles went.

I balanced it from one config file, using combat results, economy numbers, and App Store retention data to tune the difficulty curve and rewards. It also has offline progression, saving and loading, a research tree, haptics, and push notifications.

TND Card Game

1v1 online card game. Unity, C#, Unity Relay.

A card game with over 30 unique cards, a deckbuilder, and decklists you can export and import as text. To play online, one player types a code like "ABCD" and hosts, and the other types the same code and connects.

About 30 people played it regularly, and I changed cards and rules based on what they told me.
